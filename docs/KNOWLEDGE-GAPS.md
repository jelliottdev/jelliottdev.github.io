A full pinned-tree inventory now identifies 61 empty files and two nonempty byte-identical pairs eligible for the historical prefix rule; direct byte comparisons confirm all 65 grouped paths. The converter and edition manifest are absent from that tree. The historical reference-corpus pipeline has not been rerun. Freeze the edition list, conversion rules and sampling policy; document any code repair. The pinned-source synthetic audit now verifies the missing word_count argument and global-dataframe helper bug, with minimal repairs; it does not replay historical numerical results. Distinguish Latin-letter lengths from rune lengths. Do not reuse reported probabilities without reproducing their population and tail definition.
The separate [eight-language aligned comparison](../experiments/records/parallel-languages/article.md) now supplies a controlled FLORES baseline including Welsh, representation sensitivity, held-out whole-document controls, and article-cluster resampling. It does not close the historical corpus replay above. The shorter aligned-block variant now checks eight disjoint sentence blocks per language with independent histogram and decision verification. The known-reading variant now checks all nine documented sections and their pool, including an explicit amendment for LP2-56 hash contamination; some short known-English sections rank other profiles first. Genre-matched parallel text and exact target-sized calibration remain open.

## How to close a row

Link the authoritative authored page, exact inputs, runnable script, machine-readable results and relevant check. State what remains outside the result. Update the existing topic or experiment family rather than creating a duplicate per parameter or source post. New observations need searchable terms and page links; new computational claims need a reproducible bundle.

The [experiment roadmap](../experiments/ROADMAP.md) retains method-specific proposals and historical test bounds. This document tracks knowledge coverage across guides and experiments; neither is a substitute for the records themselves.
