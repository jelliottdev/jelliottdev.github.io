## Highest-value unfinished work

1. **Corpus conversion provenance and genre transfer.** The declared [full-corpus prefix comparison](../experiments/records/corpus-prefix-census/article.md) is complete: 25,906 paths and 25,844 distinct blobs source-checked; 25,565 eligible prefixes and 25,563 distinct eligible blobs; all 23 measures under path and distinct-blob weighting independently checked, retaining ties. Its article supplies the full reports, both receipts, figures and offline reproduction. This completed comparison should not remain listed as an unrun census.

The remaining historical gap is the conversion and edition provenance. The pinned source tree lacks a converter and edition manifest. The complete prefix symbol audit now checks all 25,906 paths against source bytes and the earlier census: U+16C2 is the only outside-alphabet character in the selected prefixes and occurs in 25,554 of 25,565 eligible paths (25,552 of 25,563 distinct eligible blobs). Reports and tables are preserved under `exports/corpus-symbols/`; this closes prefix-prevalence coverage, not converter provenance or full-text character coverage. Source-code controls document the historical missing word_count argument and global-dataframe helper bug with minimal repairs; they are not a replay of the original historical numerical claims. Do not reuse reported probabilities without reconstructing their population, token units and tail definitions. The new descriptive prefix comparison does not supply those missing premises.

The [eight-language aligned baseline](../experiments/records/parallel-languages/article.md), shorter-block variant, known-reading controls and exact-token variant are also complete within their declared bounds. They show why a nearest profile is not language identification: some known-English sections rank another language first, and all manuscript distances in the exact-token control exceed the corresponding observed same-language ranges. Genre-matched parallel text, historical spelling and justified letter-to-rune conversion remain open. A useful next corpus variant should address one of these gaps rather than repeat the completed ranking with a new label.

## How to close a row

The Dukotah source-review follow-ups now include separate published controls for English scorer sensitivity, decoder alignment, search maxima, image-row coverage and known-crib seed searches; see the [source review](dukotah-source-review.md) for their scope. These validate particular instruments and expose failure modes. They do not close the manuscript cipher search. In particular, the [row experiment](../experiments/records/image-row-coverage/article.md) covers eight selected images and leaves rune identity and complete transcription certification open. The [seed experiment](../experiments/records/seed-crib-controls/article.md) supplies exact cribs to synthetic fixtures; unknown alignment, uncertain cribs and the manuscript's actual generator remain unresolved.

Link the authoritative authored page, exact inputs, runnable script, machine-readable results and relevant check. State what remains outside the result. Update the existing topic or experiment family rather than creating a duplicate per parameter or source post. New observations need searchable terms and page links; new computational claims need a reproducible bundle.

The [experiment roadmap](../experiments/ROADMAP.md) retains method-specific proposals and historical test bounds. This document tracks knowledge coverage across guides and experiments; neither is a substitute for the records themselves.
