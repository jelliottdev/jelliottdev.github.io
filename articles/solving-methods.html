Reading the records

Tests, measurements and source checks answer different questions. Open a record for its result and exact limits.

What each record type means
Bounded solving test
Applies a fixed decoding, extraction or payload-retrieval rule to declared unsolved material, or excludes a specific direct-generation rule. Its result covers only those bounds.
Manuscript measurement
Measures a stated feature or statistical comparison; it does not test a complete decoding rule.
Control
Checks a search, decoder or scorer using synthetic inputs or reference text; it does not identify the cipher of an unsolved page.
Alphabet-wide analysis
Studies a declared mathematical mapping over the alphabet, rather than decoding an unsolved page.
Source or input audit
Checks image provenance, extraction or input coverage against a declared reference; it does not establish a decryption.
Historical reproduction
Replays a documented historical step with specified source material and conventions.
Method replay
Reconstructs a supplied proposed method and its choices; reproduction is not independent confirmation of a reading.
