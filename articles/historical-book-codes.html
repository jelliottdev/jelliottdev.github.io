Finding the right title is only the beginning of reproducing a book code. The actual key is a particular representation of the text: its ordering, line breaks, punctuation and numbering. This guide compares the historical Cicada examples and identifies what has been replayed here versus what remains an archival instruction.

2012: the Mabinogion was also a Reddit ordering problem

The puzzle’s working text was decoded from post titles. Substituting a convenient edition of The Lady of the Fountain can change the lines before any character is selected. The contemporary ClevCode account describes using posting order, resetting the shift key on each line, and advancing it only on letters. It also records a spacing adjustment after periods to repair displaced book-code lookups. Preserve that adjustment as a separate transformation rather than quietly editing the source.

The archived reconstruction script sorts records by creation time, handles image posts separately and suppresses repeated titles. Its anomaly log records repeated and later posts. That is evidence that copying the current visible listing is not a sufficient reconstruction procedure. It does not establish which “fourth post” a reader encountered or justify an arbitrary reorder.

A complete replay should publish a numbered table containing the post ID, creation time, original title, inclusion decision, decoded line and every selected character. Keep duplicate rejection and whitespace edits visible. The archive’s script and the contemporary spacing account need reconciliation against preserved bytes before this page can claim a complete independent replay.

2012: Blake’s plate layout is part of the key

The second-chance clue points to The Marriage of Heaven and Hell. The community wiki account identifies the work and reports the resulting onion address. The artifact archive makes the practical dependency clearer: format The Voice of the Devil as lines on the referenced full plate. It supplies a formatted text file and lookup script.

A modern paragraph transcription can preserve every word yet fail the coordinates. The useful comparison is plate image → explicitly numbered lines → selected characters. The archive reports cginiziglyaobyph.onion; that is a historical output, not a link or a newly verified service. The formatted text still needs a plate-by-plate transcription check and an independent lookup trace here.

2013: numbered verses, not screen lines

The community walkthrough identifies Crowley’s Liber AL vel Legis and explains that spaces are omitted while punctuation matters. The archived reconstruction specifies chapter and numbered-verse parsing, removing the verse labels, spaces and newlines before character selection. Browser wrapping is not the verse boundary.

Retain two outputs: the raw extraction, containing dashes in URL separator positions, and the interpreted URL with those dashes replaced by slashes. They are different stages. A missing first character should trigger comparison of the verse label, leading text and indexing origin; it is not a reason to move every coordinate until a plausible URL appears. This guide has inspected the published instructions but has not yet independently replayed every coordinate.

What every downloadable replay should contain

  1. The exact source file, its hash and provenance, plus the source image or edition where applicable.
  2. The unmodified coordinate list, with literal digits distinguished from lookups.
  3. All preprocessing rules in order, including removed characters, retained punctuation and reset boundaries.
  4. A row for every lookup: coordinate, selected source unit, character position and raw output.
  5. The raw concatenated result and any later interpretation, each labeled separately.
  6. A check that does not depend on silently repairing the answer.
