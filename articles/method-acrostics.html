An acrostic extracts initial symbols from defined units; a telestich extracts final symbols. The units might be words, lines, paragraphs or pages. Other positional extraction methods select the nth symbol or alternate between boundaries.

Major variants

Variants include first and last runes, initials after transliteration, fixed interior positions and extraction driven by a separate number sequence. These can differ when one rune represents several Latin letters.

What a test must specify

Define units and their boundaries, source layer, heading treatment, indexing origin and behavior for short units. Specify whether positions count runes, transliteration characters or bytes. Fix any alternating or repeating selection pattern before viewing the output.

How to evaluate a result

Retain a trace from each selected symbol to its source unit. Compare the complete extraction with the same procedure on controls. Do not remove inconvenient units or insert spaces only after a phrase appears without recording those extra choices.
