Visual observations

Cuneiform-like mark above the text: The compact black motif is arranged in two rows above the rune text. It occupies the upper margin, unlike its position below the text on LP2-33. Cuneiform-like is a descriptive community label, not a decoded heading. Red decimal label 5: A red decimal 5 begins a text block below the opening block. It follows the labels 1 on LP2-36 and 2, 3 and 4 on LP2-37. The opening block on this page has no new decimal label; a page boundary and a numbered-block boundary are therefore different visible features.
