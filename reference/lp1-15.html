Visual observations

Paired upright insects: Two upright insect-like figures sit below the five text rows, with their heads above long striped wings. The left figure faces inward toward the right and the right figure faces inward toward the left. Compare the single horizontal profile drawings on LP1-13 and LP1-16; this observation does not establish an exact rotation or reuse of a source image. Reclining figure along the bottom edge: A reclining human figure extends along the bottom edge, with its head to the left and feet to the right. The same general arrangement appears on LP1-14. Compare the opposite head-to-right orientation on LP2-22; no identity or source attribution is established here.
