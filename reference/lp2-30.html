Visual observations

Twelve-row black text layout: Twelve rows of black runes occupy a central text block, with broad blank margins above, below and at the sides. No separate wing, tree drawing, illustrated border or red heading is visible on this sheet. Small dots and compact punctuation clusters appear among the runes. Final-row opening dot: The final row starts with a small black dot to the left of the first rune. Additional dots occur between rune groups on the same row. The row boundary and the punctuation are separate features to retain in a transcription.
