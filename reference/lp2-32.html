Visual observations

Soft-edged tree-shaped drawing: Below the text is a dark trunk-like form with branching extensions and visibly soft edges. It forms the illustrated endpoint of the community Wing and Tree group, which begins with the wing on LP2-27. The group name is not decrypted text. Image detail and measurement: Enlarging the drawing changes its display size but does not recover missing detail. Sharpening or thresholding may change the apparent boundaries of branches; such processing should be recorded separately from the preserved original. Red dotted mark at the end of the text: A narrow red cluster of small square marks follows the final black runes. Compare its visible layout with the closing clusters on LP2-22 and the other illustrated tree page. Similar appearance and placement do not by themselves establish a cipher reset or a shared meaning.
