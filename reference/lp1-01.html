Visual observations

Paired insects beside the warning: Two upright insect-like figures flank the middle of the text, with their heads above long striped wings. They face inward toward the text. Compare the paired figures below the text on LP1-15; the placement differs, and no exact source-image reuse is established. Curled borders and central ornaments: Long curled stems run along both sides, carrying small looped and leaf-like marks. Separate ornaments sit above and below the text, each combining a central filled dot, outlined shapes and two curled arms. Similarity of the upper and lower arrangements does not by itself establish an exact geometric transformation. Red heading and red closing phrase: The warning opens with an enlarged red initial and smaller red runes. Near the end, red text begins partway through the penultimate row and continues on the final row, followed by a red punctuation cluster. The known reading gives FOR ALL IS SACRED for this closing phrase. These visible red runes are separate from the JPEG’s extracted OutGuess payload.
