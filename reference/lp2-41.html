Visual observations

Twelve runic rows within paired plant borders: Twelve rows of black runes occupy the center. Tall branching stems flank the text, with curled tips, drooping narrow leaves, root-like lower branches, and nearby groups of filled dots. No red heading or alphanumeric grid is visible. Compare the related border arrangement around the grids on LP2-49–51.
