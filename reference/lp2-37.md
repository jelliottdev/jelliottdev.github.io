Red decimal labels 2, 3 and 4: Three red decimal digits mark successive text blocks down the page: 2 at the opening block, 3 at the next, and 4 near the bottom. They continue the 1 on LP2-36 and precede the 5 on LP2-38. These are printed labels, not rune indices inferred from the text.
