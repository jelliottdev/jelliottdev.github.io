Visual observations

Stippled insect-like figure: Below the final parable is a figure formed from many small black marks. A dense central body extends downward, with scattered marks spreading into wing-like areas on both sides. This rendering differs from the outlined and shaded winged figures on LP2-53 and LP2-55. Red opening and punctuation: The text begins with an enlarged red rune beside the first three rows and smaller red runes on the opening row. Red punctuation clusters also appear in the text. Their visual color should be kept separate from the rune-only prime sums calculated from the known reading.
