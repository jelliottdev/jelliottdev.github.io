Visual observations

Mixed grid of decimal numbers and rune words: The five-by-five grid below the red CNOW THIS heading mixes decimal numbers with rune words. Keeping the numbers and replacing each word with its additive Gematria Primus value produces a square whose rows, columns and both main diagonals sum to 1033. The original tokens are not all identical under a half-turn even though their converted values are. The magic-square guide records every substitution. Small spread-wing insect between text and grid: A small winged insect-like drawing lies between the upper text and the red heading above the grid. Its wings extend to either side of a narrow central body. This is a different arrangement from the horizontal profile insects on LP1-13 and LP1-16; no species or source illustration is identified here.
