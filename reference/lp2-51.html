Visual observations

Alphanumeric grid and text arrangement: Nine rows of two-character alphanumeric entries sit above four rows of black runes. Each grid row contains eight entries. Uppercase and lowercase letters both appear; preserve their case and any leading zero when transcribing. Paired branching plant borders: Tall branching stems flank the text and grid. Both sides have curled tips, drooping narrow leaves, root-like branches at the bottom, and a nearby cluster of filled dots. Their similar arrangement is visible across LP2-49–51; exact pixel identity or a botanical identification has not been established.
