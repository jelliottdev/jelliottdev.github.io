Visual observations

Twelve runic rows within paired plant borders: Twelve rows of black runes occupy the central column. Tall curling stems flank the text, with drooping narrow leaves, root-like bases, and nearby groups of filled dots. No red heading or alphanumeric grid is visible. Compare the paired borders on LP2-41–44 and the grids on LP2-49–51.
