Visual observations

Red dotted mark at the end of the text: Six rows of black runes end with a narrow red cluster of small square marks. This closing mark is distinct from the black punctuation within the rows. Its color and placement can be recorded without assigning it a decoded meaning. Horizontal drawing with three crossbars: Below the text is a broad dark horizontal drawing crossed by three thick upright bars. Its right end narrows to a point, and small detached dark marks sit above it. This is a description of the drawing, not an identification of an object or a cipher operation. Reclining human figure along the bottom edge: A human figure stretches across the bottom of the sheet, with feet at the left and the head at the right. The face is in profile, tilted upward. The image does not establish who the figure represents, whether it is sleeping or dead, or which artwork, if any, supplied a model.
