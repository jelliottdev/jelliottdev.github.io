Gematria Primus

Alphabet reference · 29 symbols

The alphabet assigns a Latin label and a prime number to each of 29 runes. A zero-based index is useful for describing the order of the alphabet, but it is a different quantity from the prime value.

For example, ᚠ has index 0, Latin label F, and prime value 2. Labels such as TH and NG represent a single rune, not two symbols.

Reading the labels

Latin labels are transcription conventions. They do not, by themselves, decrypt the text. U/V and C/K may be represented differently in a contextual English reading. Keep the rune sequence available when comparing such readings.
