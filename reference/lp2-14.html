Visual observations

Insect in profile below the text: An insect-like figure lies below the text toward the right. Its curved body is divided into segments, a wing with internal lines rises above it, and long curved appendages extend from the rear. These visible features do not establish a species. Branching left margin: A narrow, branching drawing runs down the left side, opposite the text and insect. Its upper and lower ends spread into multiple tips. The complete page is needed to compare the two motifs and their placement.
