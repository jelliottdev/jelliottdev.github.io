Visual observations

Recorded ink colors: The stored visual survey classifies 13 rune regions as red and 249 as dark. These are measurements of the reviewed image regions, not interpretations of the colors. Oversized opening rune: The opening S rune extends alongside the first three text rows. Its rectangle is recorded separately from the ordinary first-row glyphs. Punctuation measurements: The 58 stored punctuation regions contain 56 regions with 1 connected component, 1 region with 4 connected components, 1 region with 13 connected components. 2 punctuation regions are classified red. Components are thresholded pixel groups, not a linguistic interpretation. Recounted within the stored rectangles on 19 October 2026. Cross-like drawings in opposite margins: Two dark drawings flank the text: one high in the right margin and one lower in the left. Each has a long upright stem crossed by three bars. The broadest bar is toward the top of the right-hand drawing and toward the bottom of the left-hand drawing. This describes their visible arrangement; it does not establish an exact rotation or a decoding instruction. Two small loop motifs: A small two-lobed loop sits above the first text row, and another below the final row. They are separate from the large cross-like margin drawings. Their resemblance to an infinity sign is a visual description, not an established mathematical instruction.
