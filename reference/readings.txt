The deep-web page reference	LP2–56	prime-minus-one
Method, key, and counting conventions

p = (c − (prime[j] − 1)) mod 29. Here c and p are zero-based rune indices in the 29-symbol alphabet.

The sequence starts at position 0. Prime position 0 is 2; subsequent primes are 3, 5, 7, and so on. The sequence resets at the page boundary. Non-rune characters do not advance the sequence.

Copy the runes at these zero-based positions without advancing the sequence: 56. Positions count runes across the listed section, excluding punctuation and decimal digits.

Before comparing spellings

Latin labels can use U for V and C for K, while a single rune may represent several Latin letters. These conventions can make a literal reading look unusual. Use the alphabet reference and preserve the underlying rune sequence.

The LP1-10 I/E correction is a documented transcription issue. It is shown beside the archived text instead of being silently replaced.
