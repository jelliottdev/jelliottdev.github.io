Visual observations

Decimal panel: Sixteen decimal numbers appear in four rows and four columns. 3299 is the only red entry; 708 is the only three-digit entry. Open-loop drawing: Below the number panel are two open lobes with a crossing, resembling an infinity sign. Visible gaps remain in the drawing. Its intended meaning is not established. Header: The red header contains nine runes in groups of four and five: F U L M / A EA Y OE A. Latin labels are transliterations, not plaintext.
