Visual observations

Pale tree above a winged figure: A pale gray tree outline lies between the short text block and a separate dark winged figure. The same tree appears in a darker treatment on LP2-32 and behind the 2016 message. The source comparison is R. Neil Marshman’s DeadTree.jpg photograph on Wikimedia Commons, horizontally reflected and processed. The exact processing sequence and any cipher role remain unestablished. Winged figure and curled borders: The lower figure has outspread wings with dark internal detail. Curled, branching borders run down the left and right margins. Compare the layout with LP2-53, keeping the added tree and the different amount of text in view. Red dotted mark at the end of the text: A narrow red cluster of small square marks follows the final black runes. Compare its visible layout with the closing clusters on LP2-22 and the other illustrated tree page. Similar appearance and placement do not by themselves establish a cipher reset or a shared meaning.
