## Visual observations

Curled borders on both sides: Long curved lines with inward curls frame the left and right sides of the rune column. The border is divided into upper and lower drawings. This motif recurs across LP2-03 through LP2-07; recurrence does not establish an exact pixel match or a shared cipher.

Red opening in the lower block: A short upper block ends above a broad blank gap. The lower block begins with a large red rune extending alongside three text rows, with smaller red runes at the start of its first row. This is a block opening within the page, not the page opening.
