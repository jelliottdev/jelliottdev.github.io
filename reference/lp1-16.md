## Visual observations

Left-facing insect between the instruction and numbers: A horizontal profile insect lies below the opening instruction and above the small red heading preceding the five-by-five number panel. Its head faces left and its striped wing extends right. The profile on LP1-13 faces the opposite direction.

Five-by-five number panel: Below the red heading read as CNOW THIS, the page contains five rows of five black decimal numbers. The middle row is 626, 620, 809, 620, 626. The bottom two rows reverse the order of the corresponding top two rows. This visible arrangement is separate from the question of equal row, column, or diagonal sums.

Reclining figure along the bottom edge: A reclining human figure extends along the bottom edge, with its head to the left and feet to the right. The same general arrangement appears on LP1-14. Compare the opposite head-to-right orientation on LP2-22; no identity or source attribution is established here.
