Visual observations

Winged figure beneath the text: A dark outlined figure with a narrow body and outspread wings sits below the text. The wings contain internal lines and shaded areas. Compare LP2-55 for a similar subject; this observation does not establish the same source drawing or a species. Curled side borders: Tall curled and branching forms flank the text. Their lower ends spread into root-like tips beside the winged figure. Similar placement on the two sides is visible, but exact reflection or pixel equality has not been established. Black-to-red text transition: The upper text is black. A red rune appears at the right end of a row otherwise printed in black, and the following lower rows are red. A color-based extraction would need to record that change within the row rather than assume it coincides with a row boundary. Left border: solid marks and pale rings: The left cluster contains three solid black marks and two pale outlined rings. The outlines are much fainter than the solid marks, so a dark-pixel threshold can omit them. Inspect the original colors before treating all five positions as equivalent. Right border: five solid marks: The right cluster contains five solid black marks. Compare its fill with the left cluster on this same page. The difference is visible; a binary encoding or a numerical meaning has not been established.
