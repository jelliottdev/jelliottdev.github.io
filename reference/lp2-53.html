Visual observations

Winged figure beneath the text: A dark outlined figure with a narrow body and outspread wings sits below the text. The wings contain internal lines and shaded areas. Compare LP2-55 for a similar subject; this observation does not establish the same source drawing or a species. Curled side borders: Tall curled and branching forms flank the text. Their lower ends spread into root-like tips beside the winged figure. Similar placement on the two sides is visible, but exact reflection or pixel equality has not been established. Black-to-red text transition: The upper text is black. A red rune appears at the right end of a row otherwise printed in black, and the following lower rows are red. A color-based extraction would need to record that change within the row rather than assume it coincides with a row boundary. Different fill in the border dots: The left border cluster contains three solid black marks and two pale outlined rings. The right border cluster contains five solid black marks. The distinction concerns visible fill, not a claim that the groups encode binary digits or a particular number.
