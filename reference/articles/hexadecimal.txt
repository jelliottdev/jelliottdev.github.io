Hexadecimal and byte streams

An encoding of bytes, not by itself an encryption method.

Two digits per byte
Hexadecimal uses 0–9 and a–f for values 0–15. Each digit encodes four bits; two encode one byte. Hex 41 is decimal 65, the ASCII byte for A. Leading zeros matter.
Decode a cover body
from pathlib import Path
body = Path("exports/steganography/lp1-00.body.txt").read_text()
data = bytes.fromhex(body)
assert len(data) == 991
Whitespace may separate digits. Each of the first three bodies has 1,982 hex digits and decodes to 991 bytes. Those bytes need not be readable. The next step is bytewise XOR.
Do not decode prose as hex
LP1-03 contains prose followed by hex-encoded JPEG data. Isolate the hex portion before decoding and retain the complete signed body separately from the derivative image.
