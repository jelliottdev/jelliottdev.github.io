This known section covers LP1-06 · LP1-07 · LP1-08 · LP1-09, in that order. The literal readings below preserve individual page boundaries. The contextual English at the end describes the whole section.

For the meaning and relationship of the dialogues, read The two koans: identity and the inner voice.

Method and reproduction

Shifted Atbash: worked explanation. The recorded transformation is p = (28 − c + 3) mod 29, using zero-based positions in the 29-rune alphabet.

python scripts/reproduce_reading.py first-koan
python scripts/reproduce_reading.py first-koan --trace

Run with Python 3 from the extracted reference-data bundle or a full repository checkout. The script checks its result against the shipped literal controls before printing. It uses no network or AI requests. Read the script · Exact parameters and provenance.

Inspect the arithmetic

Download the per-rune trace (CSV). Open it in a spreadsheet to follow each input rune through to its output. Each row identifies its manuscript page and zero-based rune position; key counters show when the sequence advances or pauses. Blank key fields mean that the method does not use that counter.

How to read the CSV columns · Source hashes and exact parameters (JSON). These files reproduce the known reading above; they do not test a new solution.

To regenerate the same CSV as a new file:

python scripts/reproduce_reading.py first-koan --trace --output first-koan-trace.csv

Reading the notation

Latin labels can represent multi-letter runes. Slashes, line markers, and separators retain the exported literal notation; they are not additional decoded letters. See transcription conventions and literal versus contextual text. Do not silently normalize a spelling before calculating a prime sum.

Contextual whole-section reading

This is the archived contextual rendering for all pages listed above. It may normalize or interpret rune labels; use the individual literal texts for sequence and spelling comparisons.

Show contextual English

A KOAN A MAN DECIDED TO GO AND STUDY WITH A MASTER HE WENT TO THE DOOR OF THE MASTER "WHO ARE YOU WHO WISHES TO STUDY HERE" ASKED THE MASTER THE STUDENT TOLD THE MASTER HIS NAME "THAT IS NOT WHO YOU ARE, THAT IS ONLY WHAT YOU ARE CALLED WHO ARE YOU WHO WISHES TO STUDY HERE" HE ASKED AGAIN THE MAN THOUGHT FOR A MOMENT, AND REPLIED "I AM A PROFESSOR" "THAT IS WHAT YOU DO, NOT WHO YOU ARE," REPLIED THE MASTER "WHO ARE YOU WHO WISHES TO STUDY HERE" CONFUSED, THE MAN THOUGHT SOME MORE FINALLY, HE ANSWERED, "I AM A HUMAN BEING" "THAT IS ONLY YOUR SPECIES, NOT WHO YOU ARE WHO ARE YOU WHO WISHES TO STUDY HERE", ASKED THE MASTER AGAIN AFTER A MOMENT OF THOUGHT, THE PROFESSOR REPLIED "I AM A CONSCIOUSNESS INHABITING AN ARBITRARY BODY" "THAT IS MERELY WHAT YOU ARE, NOT WHO YOU ARE WHO ARE YOU WHO WISHES TO STUDY HERE" THE MAN WAS GETTING IRRITATED "I AM," HE STARTED, BUT HE COULD NOT THINK OF ANYTHING ELSE TO SAY, SO HE TRAILED OFF AFTER A LONG PAUSE THE MASTER REPLIED, "THEN YOU ARE WELCOME TO COME STUDY" AN INSTRUCTION DO FOUR UNREASONABLE THINGS EACH DAY
