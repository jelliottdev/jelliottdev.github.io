Exclusive OR (XOR)

The bitwise operation used to combine the three cover-chain payloads.

Exclusive OR, usually written XOR or ⊕, compares bits. Two equal bits give 0; two different bits give 1. Programming languages commonly use ^ for this operation on integers.
A byte example
00001111   (hex 0f)
00110011   (hex 33)
--------
00111100   (hex 3c)
XOR is not addition with carries and is not alphabet arithmetic modulo 29. It works independently at each bit position.
The three-image chain
The signed bodies from LP1-00, LP1-01, and LP1-02 are first decoded from hexadecimal. XOR their corresponding bytes to reconstruct a signed message. XORing their ASCII hex characters would be a different operation.
How to replicate
assert len(a) == len(b) == len(c)
combined = bytes(x ^ y ^ z for x, y, z in zip(a, b, c))
# Full checked reproduction:
# python scripts/reproduce_cover_chain.py
The length check prevents silent truncation by zip. Do not pad, cycle, or trim these documented inputs. Follow the complete recipe for the resulting signature and transposition.
Useful identities and their limits
A ⊕ A = 0 and A ⊕ 0 = A. Applying the same XOR mask twice recovers the input. The three inputs can be combined in any order because XOR is associative and commutative. Those identities explain the computation; they do not establish why a particular set of manuscript files should be combined.
