Page and section ordering

Reorder whole pages or sections while accounting for the arrangements searched.

Page or section ordering changes the sequence in which material is processed. It can affect continuous key alignment, cross-page words, grid placement and statistical arrangements. Reordering whole sections is a different search from permuting every rune independently.

How quickly the choices grow

	Declared objects and choices	Number of arrangements
	Four distinct sections, arbitrary order	4! = 24
	Four distinct sections, each also reversible	4! × 2⁴ = 384
	Ten distinct pages, arbitrary order	10! = 3,628,800

These count parameter settings under the stated assumptions. Identical objects or reversal symmetries can reduce the number of distinct resulting streams. Conversely, trying several layouts, scorers and boundary rules adds further choices.

Major variants
Variants include reordering pages within a section, reordering intact sections, reversal, cyclic rotation and adjacency constraints motivated by artwork or text. Rotation may be equivalent under one score but distinct under a cipher with a fixed starting state.

Move the pages or reverse their contents?

Consider three invented pages: A contains 0 1 2, B contains 3 4, and C contains 5 6. Their labels identify source pages throughout the search. A candidate's first page need not be source page A.

	Operation	Resulting stream	What changed?
	Original A, B, C	0 1 2 | 3 4 | 5 6	Reference order
	Order C, A, B	5 6 | 0 1 2 | 3 4	Whole pages move; their contents retain their order
	Reverse page order	5 6 | 3 4 | 0 1 2	C, B, A, with each page intact
	Reverse the complete stream	6 5 | 4 3 | 2 1 0	Both page order and every page's contents reverse

The bars show page boundaries; they are not additional symbols. Rotating a page image, reading its lines backwards and reversing its entire rune stream are also different operations. A proposal needs to specify which it means.

Can the score even distinguish the orders?

Reordering intact pages preserves every single-symbol count, so whole-stream IoC and single-symbol entropy cannot choose an order. A sum of independent per-page scores cannot choose one either: its terms are merely added in a different order.

Adjacent-pair counts change only at the joins. In the original example, the joins are 2 → 3 and 4 → 5. In C, A, B they are 6 → 0 and 2 → 3. Every pair inside A, B and C remains unchanged. A whole-stream bigram score can respond to those joins, but most of its evidence is identical across candidates.

More generally, for k nonempty intact pieces containing N symbols, exactly k − 1 of the N − 1 adjacent pairs cross piece boundaries. Between any two orders, the sum of absolute changes in the pair-count table is at most 2(k − 1). This follows because at most k − 1 old joins are removed and that many new joins are added. The bound assumes unchanged contents and no inserted separators, wrapping or decoding step. Reversing pieces can change their internal pairs too.

Reflowing the stream into columns, applying a continuous keystream, or scoring longer sequences may make ordering matter much more. State that mechanism explicitly. Otherwise a large search may be optimizing a statistic that is exactly constant, or one that depends on only a few boundary pairs.

Ordering and cipher state

If a decoder restarts with the same fixed key and state on each page, moving a page does not change that page's decoded contents. If state instead continues across pages, moving an earlier page can change the key positions applied to later pages. Unequal page lengths make this especially important. See key alignment and feedback rules.

Choosing new split points is a separate search from ordering fixed pieces. For an N-symbol stream divided into exactly k nonempty contiguous pieces, there are C(N − 1, k − 1) boundary selections. For each selection there are k! orders, or k! × 2^k settings if each piece can also be reversed. These multiply to count settings, not necessarily distinct outputs. For example, choosing three pieces from seven symbols gives 15 splits and 90 split/order settings before reversals.

Do not treat an uncertain printed page join as permission to try arbitrary boundaries without counting them. Preserve the original transcription and record candidate changes separately; the page-join inventory describes the source evidence.

What a test must specify
Define the movable objects and their original order, allowed permutations, reversals, reset rules and optimization criterion. Keep page identities separate from their positions in a candidate. Say whether ordering constraints came from independent evidence or were chosen after a desirable result appeared.

Account for the ordering search
A low score after choosing the best of many arrangements should be compared with controls that receive the same search. Report tied arrangements and stable features across them. If artwork suggests an order, test the proposed relation directly rather than interpreting any favorable language statistic as confirmation.
