LP1 title page, warning, and chapter title
LP1-00, LP1-01 and LP1-02 contain signed hex bodies. Decode and XOR them to recover another signed message. Follow the complete chain; examining one body alone misses that relationship.

Welcome: LP1-03
The body begins “Let the text guide you,” followed by a good-luck message and a hex-encoded JPEG. The embedded image is distinct from the visible manuscript image. Preserve the signed body separately from the decoded derivative. Full body.

[Image: Decoded LP1-03 embedded image: a narrow row of black runes and decimal digits on white]
JPEG decoded from the signed body; a derivative, not the carrier page.
Download the decoded JPEG (15,188 bytes). To reproduce it:
from pathlib import Path
body = Path("exports/steganography/lp1-03.body.txt").read_text()
image = bytes.fromhex(body[body.index("ffd8ffe"):])
with open("embedded.jpg", "xb") as f:
    f.write(image)

LP1-10 through LP1-13
These historical instructions discuss a Tor hidden service, CGI uploads, three magic squares, and working alone. They are source documents, not current wiki setup instructions. Decorative borders use 3s on LP1-10 and LP1-11, 0s on LP1-12, and 1s on LP1-13. Similar prose does not make signed files byte-identical.
