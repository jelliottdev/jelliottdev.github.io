Method and reproduction

Prime-minus-one: worked explanation. The recorded transformation is p = (c − (prime[j] − 1)) mod 29, using zero-based positions in the 29-rune alphabet.

Start at sequence position 0. Reset the sequence at the page boundary. Non-rune characters do not consume key positions.

Copy these zero-based rune ordinals without advancing the sequence: 56. See interruptions and counting conventions; these recorded positions are not a universal rule for other pages.
