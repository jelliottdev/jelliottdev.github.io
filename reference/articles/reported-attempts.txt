Grid bytes as a QR or data-matrix image
Related work only · Radix changes and byte encodings · LP2-15, LP2-49, LP2-50, LP2-51
Reported approach. Convert the LP2-49–51 base-60 pairs to fixed-width bits and plot them as a black-white matrix; the report also describes earlier LP2-15 and solved-page number layouts, a mistaken base-62 pass, and a corrected bit layout.
Current archive coverage. The grid guide reproduces base-60 bytes and one fixed 64 by 32 bit rendering. The direct-byte experiment screens text and headers; neither reproduces every matrix shape or image-recognition attempt in this report.
Replay requirement. Recover the exact corrected bit stream and plotting script, declare widths, bit order, padding, source transcription and image acceptance rule, then run the same detector on matched random bit grids.

LP2-15 word sums, nearest-prime distances and reverse-add palindromes
Partly replayed · Gematria and rune-sum mappings · LP2-15, LP2-16
Reported approach. Map each word's GP sum to its distance from a nearest prime, concatenate those distances under selected zero separators, and add the resulting decimal number to its digit reversal. The thread reports several palindromes and later corrects an earlier 46-digit claim to 53 digits.
Current archive coverage. The selected 53-digit decimal equality is now reproduced, with a no-carry explanation and a fixed-digit shuffle control. The archive also checks row sums and the LP2-15 number square. None of these records derives the reported word-level nearest-prime sequence from the manuscript.
Replay requirement. Freeze the page transcription, word segmentation, nearest-prime tie rule, leading-zero handling and exact grouping before calculation. Derive every digit from the runes, enumerate all eligible sections and zero groupings, then compare the full selection process with matched controls.
