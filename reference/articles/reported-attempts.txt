Grid bytes as a QR or data-matrix image
Related work only · Radix changes and byte encodings · LP2-15, LP2-49, LP2-50, LP2-51
Reported approach. Convert the LP2-49–51 base-60 pairs to fixed-width bits and plot them as a black-white matrix; the report also describes earlier LP2-15 and solved-page number layouts, a mistaken base-62 pass, and a corrected bit layout.
Current archive coverage. The grid guide reproduces base-60 bytes and one fixed 64 by 32 bit rendering. The direct-byte experiment screens text and headers; neither reproduces every matrix shape or image-recognition attempt in this report.
Replay requirement. Recover the exact corrected bit stream and plotting script, declare widths, bit order, padding, source transcription and image acceptance rule, then run the same detector on matched random bit grids.

LP2-40 FOR EVERY, reflected florals and a Möbius running key
Related work only · Cipher composition and ordered pipelines · LP2-40
Reported approach. Treat FOR EVERY as a candidate red-header reading, combine a shift of 18 and Atbash, interpret floral details as 13 and 31, skip a chosen rune, then investigate a Möbius-directed running/autokey and trigram structure.
Current archive coverage. The cited header, visual interpretation and composite key rule have not been replayed together in the experiment registry. Other running-key and crib records test different inputs or rules.
Replay requirement. Snapshot the changing spreadsheet, separate a fixed FOR EVERY crib test from the artwork-derived parameters, specify the complete key clock and skipped-rune rule, and score the untouched continuation against matched controls.

Six F interrupters and periodic IoC on LP2-54–55
Partly replayed · Keystream interruptions and reset rules · LP2-54, LP2-55
Reported approach. Enumerate 64 settings of six F positions under two ciphertext-autokey interruption rules and compare periodic IoC, especially a reported period-13 peak.
Current archive coverage. Two published LP2-54–55 records test specific skip and reset variants. They do not verify every original plot or the reported 64-configuration periodic-IoC comparison.
Replay requirement. Recover the original code/graphs or transcribe both exact recurrences, fix the F coordinate list and IoC denominator, replay all 64 masks, and compare the selected maximum with position-matched interrupter controls.

Alberti disks, rule transitions and branching on LP2-39
Not replayed · Moving disks, rotors and evolving alphabets · LP2-39
Reported approach. Apply cover-derived disk correspondences and four rotation rules, a reported 5-6-3-11 sequence, and dot-triggered branches to LP2-39 rows. The transcript refers to separate method and test PDFs not present in the supplied text.
Current archive coverage. No dedicated moving-alphabet experiment reproduces this exact rule set. General disk and compass guides do not validate the reported branched readings.
Replay requirement. Obtain the two referenced PDFs and diagram, freeze both disk alphabets and each state transition before reading output, enumerate every branch rather than selected words, and require known-reading and shuffled controls.

Large line gaps, punctuation and JPEG size
Related work only · Source verification and transcription audits
Reported approach. Measure unusually large gaps between rune rows, associate them with punctuation or number panels, and compare JPEG sizes. The report gives 109 versus 73 pixels for a typical gap and a different parable-page measurement.
Current archive coverage. The wiki has page-region observations and a JPEG-size audit, but no full source-pinned replay of the report's exact gap-coordinate census or its claimed punctuation association.
Replay requirement. Pin the exact JPEG versions, define row bounding boxes and gap statistic for every page, verify the reported 109/73 values and exceptional page, then compare the punctuation association without selecting pages after inspection.

Proposed mechanisms for scarce adjacent doublets
Partly replayed · Autokey and ciphertext feedback
Reported approach. Compare additive, Beaufort and multiplicative ciphertext feedback with deletion, insertion and reordering as distinct explanations for the low doublet rate.
Current archive coverage. The archive measures doublets and tests uniform additive keys and one-rune deletion. It does not treat this mechanism list as one tested cipher or cover all listed variants.
Replay requirement. Split the list into separately bounded families; for each, state the operation and zero handling, predict the observed doublet count on known readings, and score any output with held-out text rather than doublet fit alone.

729-cell cross-section inversion and fractional rune choices
Related work only · Geometric constructions and navigation · LP2-00, LP2-01, LP2-02
Reported approach. Use 729 divided by a rune's ordinal, reduce modulo 29, then choose among base and line-dependent neighboring rune labels from the fractional part. The post allows further letter rearrangement and removal to interpret output.
Current archive coverage. The published 27 by 27 totient-map replay is a different algorithm; it does not reproduce this 729/ordinal/fraction rule.
Replay requirement. Define the zero-ordinal case, precise decimal/rounding rule, line numbering, branch choice and allowed postprocessing; emit the full rune trace and all candidate outputs before evaluating against predeclared language controls.

Möbius-of-totient zero mask and proposed LP2-00 reading
Related work only · Bounded arithmetic expressions · LP2-00
Reported approach. Assign each rune prime p the value μ(φ(p)), preserve zero-category positions across a candidate decoding, and use word-list search plus thematic word selection to propose a reading of LP2-00.
Current archive coverage. The archive documents totient shifts and word-sum candidates but has not reproduced this zero-mask constraint or the claimed first-page reading.
Replay requirement. Verify the mask against every known ciphertext/plaintext pair, pin the dictionary and rune spelling rules, count all candidate words per position, then test the proposed sentence on untouched rows and matched random masks.

JPEG byte histograms and alleged null-heavy page classes
Not replayed · Source verification and transcription audits

Current archive coverage. The archive pins JPEGs and audits metadata and file sizes, but has not reproduced the original per-byte histogram classification on the exact cited source files.
Replay requirement. Pin hashes of the cited JPEG versions, compute all 256 byte-value counts per file, specify a quantitative grouping statistic and JPEG encoding metadata, then compare solved and unsolved page sets without manual histogram labels.
