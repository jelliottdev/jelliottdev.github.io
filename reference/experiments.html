Count matching ordered pairs in each column and add them. Divide by the total number of ordered pairs available within all columns:\n\n```\npooled IoC(k) = sum over columns and symbols of n(c,s)*(n(c,s)-1)\n                / sum over columns of N(c)*(N(c)-1)\n```\n\nThis weights columns by their number of pairs; it is not the unweighted mean of column IoCs.
