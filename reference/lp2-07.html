Visual observations

Curled borders on both sides: Long curved lines with inward curls frame the left and right sides of the rune column. The border is divided into upper and lower drawings. This motif recurs across LP2-03 through LP2-07; recurrence does not establish an exact pixel match or a shared cipher. Red closing line: Below the black text is a short red line, with compact red punctuation at its ends. It is visually separate from the five much larger black dots lower on the page. Five large dots below the text: Five separated black dots lie below the red line in a loose arrangement, not a single horizontal row. Small specks beside the upper border curves are a different set of marks and should not be silently included in this count.
