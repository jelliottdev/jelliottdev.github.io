Visual observations

Cuneiform-like mark below the text: A compact black mark occupies two rows below the final rune row. The community name cuneiform-like describes its appearance, not an established historical sign or translation. The inspection window includes surrounding whitespace. Three separated black dots: Three large black dots form a horizontal row between the upper and lower text blocks. These are visually distinct from the much smaller punctuation clusters within the text. Enlarged red opening runes: Each of the two text blocks begins with a large red rune extending alongside its first three text rows. Smaller red runes and red punctuation clusters also appear at the starts of the blocks. This records placement and color, not an instruction to extract them.
