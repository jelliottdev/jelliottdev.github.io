## Visual observations

Soft-edged tree-shaped drawing: Below the text is a dark, soft-edged version of the tree also used on LP2-55 and in the 2016 message. The source comparison is R. Neil Marshman’s DeadTree.jpg photograph on Wikimedia Commons, horizontally reflected and processed. The exact processing sequence has not been reconstructed. This page ends the community Wing and Tree group, which begins on LP2-27; that group name is not decrypted text.

Image detail and measurement: Enlarging the drawing changes its display size but does not recover missing detail. Sharpening or thresholding may change the apparent boundaries of branches; such processing should be recorded separately from the preserved original.

Red dotted mark at the end of the text: A narrow red cluster of small square marks follows the final black runes. Compare its visible layout with the closing clusters on LP2-22 and the other illustrated tree page. Similar appearance and placement do not by themselves establish a cipher reset or a shared meaning.
