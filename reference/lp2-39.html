Visual observations

Cuneiform-like mark above the text: The compact black motif is arranged in two rows above the rune text. It occupies the upper margin, unlike its position below the text on LP2-33. Cuneiform-like is a descriptive community label, not a decoded heading. Three separated black dots: Three large black dots lie horizontally in the gap between the upper text block and the lower block. They are separate from the smaller punctuation marks within the rune text. Compare the three-dot row on LP2-33. Enlarged red rune in the lower block: A large red rune begins the lower text block and extends alongside its first three rows. Smaller red runes follow it at the start of the first row. This color and scale change is visible, but does not by itself specify a decryption rule.
