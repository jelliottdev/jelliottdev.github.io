## Visual observations

Curled borders on both sides: Long curved lines with inward curls frame the left and right sides of the rune column. The border is divided into upper and lower drawings. This motif recurs across LP2-03 through LP2-07; recurrence does not establish an exact pixel match or a shared cipher.

Continuous black text column: The central column contains black rune text, without the enlarged red opening runes seen on LP2-03 and LP2-06. Compare complete images before assuming that matching border drawings imply identical text segmentation.
