Visual observations

Opposed insect-like figures beside the text: Elongated insect-like figures flank the rune column on both sides. On each side, the upper and lower winged forms face opposite vertical directions, with long narrow lines between them. Record the side and orientation before comparing details; resemblance alone does not establish that the two sides are exact reflections.
