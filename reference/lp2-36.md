## Visual observations

Cuneiform-like mark above the text: The compact black motif is arranged in two rows above the rune text. It occupies the upper margin, unlike its position below the text on LP2-33. Cuneiform-like is a descriptive community label, not a decoded heading.

Red decimal label 1: A red decimal 1 appears at the left of a new text block below the opening block. It is a printed digit, not a Gematria Primus rune. The numbered sequence continues on LP2-37 and LP2-38; no cipher function is established by its presence.
