## Visual observations

Five large dots above the text: Five separated black dots occupy the space above the opening text. They are much larger than the punctuation marks within the rune rows. Their arrangement should be recorded from the image rather than inferred from the community group name Mayfly.

Enlarged red opening rune: The opening rune is enlarged and red, extending beside the first three text rows. Smaller red runes appear at the start of the first row, followed by black text. The insect-like side figures visible on LP2-24 through LP2-26 are absent here.
