Visual observations

Cuneiform-like mark above the text: The compact black motif is arranged in two rows above the rune text. It occupies the upper margin, unlike its position below the text on LP2-33. Cuneiform-like is a descriptive community label, not a decoded heading. Red decimal labels 2, 3 and 4: Three red decimal digits mark successive text blocks down the page: 2 at the opening block, 3 at the next, and 4 near the bottom. They continue the 1 on LP2-36 and precede the 5 on LP2-38. These are printed labels, not rune indices inferred from the text.
