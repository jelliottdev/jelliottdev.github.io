Visual observations

Five large black dots below the text: Five filled black dots form a loose arrangement below the final two runic rows. They are much larger than the small red punctuation clusters within the text. This is another five-dot arrangement to compare with LP2-07 and LP2-23; the count alone does not identify a symbol, reading order or decoding instruction. Hexadecimal block between runic passages: Five rows of Latin hexadecimal characters sit between an upper runic passage and two lower runic rows. The characters are printed directly on the page. The target guide joins those rows into one 128-character string while retaining their original display breaks. Red opening and small red closing marks: An enlarged red initial stands beside the upper passage, with smaller red runes on its first row. Small red punctuation clusters close the upper and lower runic passages. The large black dots farther below are a separate visual feature.
