Visual observations

Curled and branching side borders: Long black stems frame both sides of the text, combining large curls with smaller curled branches and root-like ends. A hanging cluster of thin branches occupies the middle of each border. The resemblance between the margins is visible; an exact reflection or reuse of the same source image has not been established by this observation. Left border: solid marks and pale rings: The left cluster contains three solid black marks and two pale outlined rings. The outlines are much fainter than the solid marks, so a dark-pixel threshold can omit them. Inspect the original colors before treating all five positions as equivalent. Right border: five solid marks: The right cluster contains five solid black marks. Compare its fill with the left cluster on this same page. The difference is visible; a binary encoding or a numerical meaning has not been established. Red opening beside three rows: An enlarged red opening rune extends alongside the first three text rows. Smaller red runes follow on the first row, ending at a compact red punctuation cluster before black text continues. The pale tree and winged figure associated with this two-page community group appear on LP2-55, not on this page.
