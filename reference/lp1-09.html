Visual observations

Bearded figure above a scroll-like frame: A central bearded human figure rises above the rectangular text frame. Spiral ornaments sit on either side near the shoulders, and the bottom of the frame has a shaded, uneven edge with curled sides. This arrangement appears on LP1-06 through LP1-09. It differs from the paired long-haired figures framing LP1-10 through LP1-13. No identity or source artwork is established here. Left-facing insect between dialogue and instruction: Below the closing dialogue and its red punctuation cluster is a horizontal insect-like figure. Its head faces left and its striped wing extends right. Beneath it, an enlarged red initial and smaller red heading introduce the instruction in the known reading. Compare the left-facing profile on LP1-16; visual similarity alone does not establish exact source-image reuse.
