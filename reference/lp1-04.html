Visual observations

Leafy border around the Welcome text: Thick curving branches surround the central text column. Dense leaf-shaped forms occupy the top, while larger open loops and scattered leaves descend along both margins and curve beneath the text. LP1-03 and LP1-04 share this broad arrangement. It is distinct from the narrow single-margin branching drawings on LP2-08 through LP2-14; no plant species or cipher operation is established by the resemblance. Small red instruction beneath the main text: A single line of much smaller red runes sits beneath the main text, just above the lower border. The known reading gives AN INSTRUCTIAN. COMMAND YOUR OWN SELF. Its small size makes it easy to miss in a page thumbnail. It is visible page text, separate from any hidden content extracted from an image.
