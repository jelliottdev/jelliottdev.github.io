Visual observations

Curled borders on both sides: Long curved lines with inward curls frame the left and right sides of the rune column. The border is divided into upper and lower drawings. This motif recurs across LP2-03 through LP2-07; recurrence does not establish an exact pixel match or a shared cipher. Black text between the borders: Black rune text fills the central column. A horizontal gap separates the upper text from the lower rows. Unlike LP2-03, this page does not show enlarged red opening runes.
