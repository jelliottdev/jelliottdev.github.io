## Visual observations

Bearded figure above a scroll-like frame: A central bearded human figure rises above the rectangular text frame. Spiral ornaments sit on either side near the shoulders, and the bottom of the frame has a shaded, uneven edge with curled sides. This arrangement appears on LP1-06 through LP1-09. It differs from the paired long-haired figures framing LP1-10 through LP1-13. No identity or source artwork is established here.

Red opening of the first koan: An enlarged red initial stands beside the opening rows, with smaller red runes on the first row. The known reading identifies the opening as A Koan. The rest of the page continues the dialogue inside the frame.
