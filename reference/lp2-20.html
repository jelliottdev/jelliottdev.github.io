Visual observations

Twelve black rows within blank margins: Twelve rows of black runes occupy a central text block with broad blank margins. No red heading, crossed-loop drawing, or separate illustrated border is visible on this sheet. The community Mobius grouping describes its location in LP2, not a motif repeated on every page. Dotted cluster before the seventh row: A compact dotted cluster precedes the first rune of the seventh row. A smaller dot also precedes the last row. These marks are distinct from the rune strokes and should be recorded separately in a punctuation analysis.
