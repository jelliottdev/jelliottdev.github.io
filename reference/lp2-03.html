Visual observations

Curled borders on both sides: Long curved lines with inward curls frame the left and right sides of the rune column. The border is divided into upper and lower drawings. This motif recurs across LP2-03 through LP2-07; recurrence does not establish an exact pixel match or a shared cipher. Two text blocks with red opening runes: The text is divided into an upper and a lower block. Each begins with an enlarged red rune beside its first three rows, followed by smaller red runes on the opening row before the text turns black. The two blocks are separated by a broad blank gap.
