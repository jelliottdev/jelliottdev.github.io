The **107 archived scripts are not a replayed set**. `tools/legacy/README.md` says many read pre-2026-09-29 misaligned page data; a static scan finds 78 with literal old page paths and 50 with parent-directory assumptions (overlapping sets), and most are optimizers with outputs archived separately. They require an individual method-and-input audit, canonical input migration, declared success rule, and controls before any result can be used. Their file count is neither 107 independent ideas nor evidence that their historical outcomes hold. The two other top-level `tools/*.py` programs, `rebuild_page_files.py` and `add_contributor.py`, are respectively a corpus maintenance and contributor utility, not decryption campaigns.

J/K/L is published; H/O is published. Legacy scripts should be triaged for runnable exact methods, without turning archived optimizer output into negative evidence.

One finite legacy method is published separately as `wulfic-keyword-prime-layer-grid`: the 16-keyword, 103-second-stream, four-sign additive grid from `tools/legacy/combined_cipher_test.py`. Its old `page_21–54` rune files match *current* LP2-04–37, not LP2-21–54. This mapping was independently checked by nonempty length and GP-rune SHA256 for all 34 files; the first wrong-target exploratory run is explicitly discarded in the draft's input-mapping amendment. The corrected 224,128-cell grid, known-reading control and whole-search shuffles are complete. This bounded adaptation does not make the other 106 archived scripts trustworthy or covered.
