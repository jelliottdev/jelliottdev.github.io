## Why the rest is queued differently

The P02, P24, P25, anchored and GPU hillclimbers form connected optimizer lineages. Their downstream scripts inspect candidate outputs selected by earlier searches. The P25 first-stage offset search above does not validate its later substitution optimizer. The [dependency audit](wulfic-optimizer-dependency-audit-2026-10-23.md) distinguishes **genuinely absent** public GPU/anchored checkpoints and selected outputs from corpora that merely moved to `data/corpora/`. Before replay, recover a pinned checkpoint where one exists, original target bytes, score/training data, complete stopping/selection rule, and independent control. Key-validation scripts cannot verify a claimed plaintext if they rely on the same assumed anchors that generated it. Source code comments calling a result “verified” or “breakthrough” are claims to examine, not adopted evidence.

The upstream [legacy README](https://github.com/Wulfic/Cicada3301-Liber_Primus/blob/bf89d3d13aad2b8f2c95ab195cf1b092f250775c/tools/legacy/README.md) warns that older rune/image pairings and moved relative paths make the historical outputs unreliable. The site-to-source page mapping is independently recorded in `research/source-intake/wulfic-legacy-page-map-2026-10-23.json`; it validates the *current pinned files*, not every earlier run.
