With the fixed three-window pattern (starts separated by 29 F occurrences, final window ending 116 positions after the first start), **350** starts are possible; only start 182 has the exact ordered triad. Including LP1 yields 537 F occurrences and different sums; that demonstrates why the LP2-only scope must be explicit. This checks arithmetic and conditional uniqueness, **not** the chance of obtaining a selected pattern after searching many window sizes, message splits, axes, coordinate maps or origin rules.
