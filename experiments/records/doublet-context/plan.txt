Question: Do the values immediately before and after the 86 archived LP2 doublets, and the ciphertext symbols at those doublets, show extra placement structure beyond the known zero mask and each page's nonzero difference inventory?
Input: archived LP2-00--55 text and the 29-rune alphabet in data/index.json. Hash all 56 texts and the index. Keep headings, filter non-runes, calculate within-page differences modulo 29 only.
Null/control: fix each page's first ciphertext rune, its observed zero-difference positions, and the multiset of its nonzero differences. Uniformly permute only the nonzero differences within each page, then reconstruct ciphertext by cumulative addition. Seed 330122 and make 5,000 trials. This is a conditional order control; it is not an English plaintext simulator or a test of the marginal difference histogram.
Diagnostics, selected before running this control: (1) collision pairs among the 86 ciphertext symbols at doublet positions, sum over symbol bins of c(c-1)/2; (2) the sum of squared differences between counts of the nonzero residues immediately preceding and following zero-difference positions, divided by their pooled count plus one in each residue. Exclude nonexistent page-edge neighbors and zero neighboring differences; record exclusions explicitly. Show all 29 event-symbol counts and all 28 before/after counts, not only summary statistics.
For each diagnostic report the central 95% of seeded conditional trials and inclusive counts at least/at most observed. Do not claim prospective p-values: the zero mask and interest in local context were known before this analysis, and the two diagnostics are not independent of the existing difference histogram and placement analyses.
Algebraic scope: in C_i=C_(i-1)+K_i(P_i-28) mod 29 with nonzero K, a doublet locates a proposed plaintext EA. It does not label the ciphertext doublet rune as EA. If the following plaintext symbol is non-EA and K is uniform nonzero, the next difference is uniform on 1..28; the next ciphertext rune cannot equal the doublet rune. A triple requires consecutive plaintext EA under that equation. The conditional control fixes the observed mask and cannot test that triple count.
