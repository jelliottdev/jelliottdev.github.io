## Result

**A constant-rate rotating alphabet did not make any complete unsolved LP2 page read like the archived English passages.** We tested **44,660 outputs**: 812 distinct progressive shifts on each of 55 rune-bearing pages. None reached the fixed −5.5 English-score threshold. The best was **−6.426**, on the short 66-rune LP2-49 page with step `k = 17` and starting offset `b = 11`.

Selection matters: **70 of 199** controls made by shuffling runes within each page produced an equal or higher best score after the **same complete search**. This is a narrow result for a linear position clock, not a verdict on Alberti disks or every changing-alphabet method.

![The best LP2 progressive-shift score lies within complete-search shuffled controls and well below the weakest whole known reading.](experiments/records/wholepage-progressive-caesar/scores.svg)

## The exact rule

At rune position `i`, counted from zero at the start of each page, we decode `Pᵢ = Cᵢ + k·i + b (mod 29)`. Only runes advance `i`; headings are included. We tested every **nonzero step** `k = 1…28` and every offset `b = 0…28`. A negative step is another value of `k` modulo 29, and changing the starting phase only changes `b`, so neither needs a duplicated case. Constant shifts (`k = 0`) were already covered by the [static affine census](#/experiments/wholepage-affine-gp29). LP2-50 has no runes and was excluded.

We validated the score before inspecting LP2. All **nine complete archived readings** exceeded −5.5 and beat more than 99% of 200 rune-order shuffles of their own text. After enciphering each reading with the fixed schedule `k = 7, b = 13`, its true plaintext ranked first among all 812 tested schedules **nine times out of nine**. These controls show that a comparable English reading generated by this rule would register; they do not prove LP2's hidden language or structure.

For each of 199 null trials, we shuffled positions independently within every page, preserving its length and rune counts, then retained the single highest score over all **55 × 812** outputs. This controls selection over both page and schedule under that positional model. It is not a simulation of the manuscript's encryption. The tested schedules are variants of one method, not thousands of independent hypotheses.

## Reproduce and limits

The [plan](experiments/records/wholepage-progressive-caesar/plan.json) pins all page transcriptions, solved readings, alphabet and score model by SHA-256. The [case ledger](experiments/records/wholepage-progressive-caesar/cases.jsonl) contains every schedule, score and decoded-index hash; [results](experiments/records/wholepage-progressive-caesar/results.json) retain all 199 global null maxima. Run `python experiments/records/wholepage-progressive-caesar/run.py --check`, then `python experiments/records/wholepage-progressive-caesar/check.py` for an independent reconstruction. Both full searches use NumPy and Numba; fixed outputs are also checked by a separate ordinary Latin-string scorer.

The position clock here increments once per rune and resets at each page. It does not test word or line resets, nonlinear clocks, extra key layers, mixed alphabets, transposition, non-English text, or the branching disk proposal's unspecified transitions.

The ledger retains decoded-output hashes, not decoded text.
