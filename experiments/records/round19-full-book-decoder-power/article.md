# Can this decoder keep a known key aligned across the full book?

The [Round 19 source test](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/tree/f08b20b632d91209ddbaa747e8e2f23df0c78ba1/liber-primus/analysis/round19/I1) encrypts **12,956 runes of known text**, deliberately skipping some key draws, then gives the correct key to two decoders. One permits the source's one-draw skip; the other permits a broader drift rule. This is a long-message power check for the decoder, not an LP2 key search.

![At full-book length, both decoders recover more than 99.8% of a planted message with the correct key; wrong-key controls recover less than 5%.](experiments/records/round19-full-book-decoder-power/full-book-power.svg)

**Result:** With the correct key, median exact rune recovery was **99.98%** for the source's exact skip rule and **99.82%** for its permissive drift rule, across three planted seeds per mode. Every correct-key run cleared the source's 90% recovery and −5.5 language-score bars. The one wrong-key control per mode recovered **3.77%** and **4.88%**, respectively. These controls show the two decoders can follow this specific skip construction over a full-book length when given the right key. They do not establish the correct key or rule for LP2.

The exact replay matched **all eight archived source rows** on every field except elapsed wall time. It used the source's `self_reliance.txt` English text, GP29 converter, SHA-generated key, 0.83 doublet-suppression setting and width-400 beam. The modes differ: `repo_ms3` allows up to three source-style skipped draws; `drift_l8` allows additional free drift with its source-defined cost. These are not interchangeable decoder rules. The earlier [short-message control](experiments/records/round19-decoder-baseline-control/article.md) tested six decoders at 240 and 400 runes; this record verifies the separate **12,956-rune** source arm.

This is a retrospective source replay: the eight archived outcomes were known before its [plan](experiments/records/round19-full-book-decoder-power/plan.json) was saved. It validates a long planted-message gate, not a false-positive rate for searching unknown keys or another cipher model. A readable score on synthetic English-derived text does not identify the LP2 plaintext.

The experiment ZIP includes the pinned source code and data under its MIT license. To rerun the eight decodes from the extracted folder, use `python experiments/records/round19-full-book-decoder-power/run.py --nproc 6`; the runner stages a disposable source copy and does not rewrite the archived [source output](experiments/records/round19-full-book-decoder-power/source/liber-primus/analysis/round19/I1/out_book.json). Use `python experiments/records/round19-full-book-decoder-power/run.py --check` to verify the saved replay, source rows and independently recounted gate summaries without rerunning the beam.
