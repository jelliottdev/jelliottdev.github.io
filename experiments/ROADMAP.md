# Initial experiment priorities

Internal work queue. This is not a published result or a combined reader-facing article. Each approach needs its own bounded experiment page; group variations within the relevant family. Do not make ten empty placeholders or present plans as tests.

Exact-repeat baseline: length-three overlapping windows on LP2-00–55; sum within-page matching-start pairs. Observed 55, with 798/999 whole-page fixed-count shuffle totals at least that large; upper-tail p=0.799. Standalone record includes positions, page CSV and histogram. No alternative lengths, between-page comparisons or per-page significance tested. Prior descriptive LP2-31 result disclosed.

Within-segment reversal now has its own record in the reading-route family. Two predeclared line-boundary conventions give 110 distinct page outputs and zero matches to nine fixed beginnings. Full traces audited with regex spans and reflected positions; complete rerun agrees. Word-order reversal and broader language ranking remain untested.

The fixed-alphabet warning-prefix baseline covers a single narrowly defined criterion relevant to items 1–3. It is not a general completion of these subjects. The periodic-IoC baseline covers LP2-31 periods 2–20 only. Continue building independently useful records, without inflating the registry with duplicates or a record per parameter value.

OutGuess default-key baseline: all 77 declared images attempted twice; ten controls reproduce, 21 outputs remain unverified, five retrievals are rejected and 41 abnormal exits remain unresolved. Next useful variant: same key and images with another documented build. Do not classify failed extractions as clean negative results.

Known Vigenère key baseline: two recorded vectors, all 21 phases, both directions, LP2-00–55; 2352 candidates, nine fixed 12-rune openings, no exact matches. No interruption search or general language score. Treat new alignment hypotheses and key lists as declared variants.

Periodic-IoC family now has a within-row-shuffle variant: same LP2-31 input, score and periods; p=0.246 versus whole-page p=0.214. Both above 0.05. This is a declared sensitivity comparison, not independent confirmation.

Book-cipher control: frozen Emerson PDF re-extracted with pypdf 6.19.0; all 50 paragraphs and 15 lookups plus seven literals reproduce. Known-result historical control only; unsolved-page applications remain untested.

Prime-row baseline: 594 eligible archived rows, 76 prime sums; within-page shuffles preserving row lengths give upper-tail p=0.844. Global excess only; LP2-50 contributes no runic rows. No per-page significance or emirp test.

Reading-route baseline: sixteen row/ordinal-column route configurations on 55 nonempty pages (LP2-50 zero runes); 880 cases, nine fixed openings, zero matches. Full coordinate permutations saved. Does not test physical alignment, arbitrary permutations or combined substitution.

Prime-minus-one baseline: 29 offsets, both directions, 55 nonempty LP2 pages; 3190 candidates and 28710 fixed-opening comparisons, zero matches. All candidates audited; first 56 LP2-56 control runes reproduce. Other schedules belong in separate declared variants, not a combined ten-approach article.

Ordinary-prime variant: only the schedule changes from the prime-minus-one baseline; all 3190 cases retested and compared. Zero fixed-opening matches. Every candidate differs by its sign modulo 29, so an all-constant-shifts search would duplicate coverage. Separate result page, same family.

Fibonacci variant: same search settings, F_0=0. Verified residue period 14; all 3190 configurations reproduce but yield 1540 distinct page outputs. No nine-opening matches. Offset expansion alone adds no coverage. Consecutive-integer totients are now recorded separately; broader justified plaintext criteria remain open.

Consecutive-integer totient variant: phi(1) onward, same 29 offsets and two signs. All 3190 outputs distinct within page; zero fixed-opening matches. Every totient independently checked by gcd counting. Phi of primes is the prime-minus-one baseline, not another result. Other totient inputs and boundary rules remain untested.
