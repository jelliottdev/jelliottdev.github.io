# Initial experiment priorities

Internal work queue. This is not a published result or a combined reader-facing article. Each approach needs its own bounded experiment page; group variations within the relevant family. Do not make ten empty placeholders or present plans as tests.

1. Direct reading and Atbash reuse.
2. Constant shifts of the 29-rune alphabet.
3. Whole-stream, row-wise and word-wise reversal (separate conventions).
4. Repeating-key Vigenère with a declared key list and boundary rules.
5. Prime, prime-minus-one, Fibonacci and totient schedules (named variants).
6. Layout-based transpositions with coordinate traces.
7. Periodicity and repeated-pattern tests with selection-aware null comparisons.
8. Rune sums, prime indices and emirps with fixed boundaries and null models.
9. Book ciphers using evidenced references and exact editions.
10. Image/steganography checks on original bytes, with tool versions and payload verification.

The fixed-alphabet warning-prefix baseline covers a single narrowly defined criterion relevant to items 1–3. It is not a general completion of these subjects. The periodic-IoC baseline covers LP2-31 periods 2–20 only. Continue building independently useful records, without inflating the registry with duplicates or a record per parameter value.
